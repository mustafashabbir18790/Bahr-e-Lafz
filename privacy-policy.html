Privacy Policy

Bahr-e-Lafz — last updated 26 July 2026

Bahr-e-Lafz is a dictionary and meter-lookup tool for Urdu poetry. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

What this app collects

Nothing. Bahr-e-Lafz does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. All searches happen entirely on your device — the word list is bundled into the app itself, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Offline storage

The app caches its own files on your device so it keeps working without an internet connection. This cached data stays on your device and is never uploaded anywhere. You can clear it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings.

Third-party services

The app loads two typefaces (Noto Nastaliq Urdu and JetBrains Mono) from Google Fonts when first opened online. Loading these fonts may share your device's IP address with Google, subject to Google's own privacy policy. No other third-party service is used.

Children's privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this app ever starts collecting data in the future, this page will be updated to reflect that before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app's developer through the Play Store listing.
